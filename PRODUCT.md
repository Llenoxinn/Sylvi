# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who run print shops in the Philippines and the staff at their counters. Walk-in customers ask for ID photos (1x1, 2x2 and passport, for board exams, school IDs, NBI clearance, job and visa applications) and send their photo by Messenger or email. The shop's job is to turn that photo into a correctly sized, cut-ready print sheet quickly, many times a day.

## Product Purpose

Sylvi replaces laying out ID photos by hand in Microsoft Word. A worker adds the customer's photo; Sylvi finds the face and crops each size, packs every photo onto the paper loaded in the printer, marks every cut, and exports a print-ready file at true size. Success is a sheet that prints right the first time and is cut in a few straight trimmer passes.

## Positioning

Free, with no trial, watermark or sign-up. It runs in the browser, works offline after the first visit, and customer photos never leave the computer (RA 10173). Built-in packages A to D match the menus shops already post, plus whole-sheet fills, and every layout splits edge to edge so the trimmer always cuts straight.

## Operating Context

Used at a busy shop counter on whatever computer the shop has, often older PCs, printing on photo paper (3R, 4R, 5R) or bond (A4, short, long) with inkjet printers set to Actual size (100%). Several customers can share one sheet, each with their own package and crop. Shops can sign in with Google to keep their packages and shop details on every computer they use.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Automatic face-based crop per photo size, then manual crop, brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth and sharpness.
- Paper: 3R, 4R, 5R, A4, short bond (Letter), long bond (8.5 × 13 in) or custom; auto, portrait or landscape; adjustable margin.
- Cut guides: crop marks, cut lines or a white border, with an optional 1-inch check line.
- Export: PDF, PNG or JPG at 300 DPI, or print from the browser.
- Recent jobs stay on the device and delete themselves (24 hours by default).
- Optional sign-in syncs packages and shop settings only, never photos.

## Brand Commitments

The name is Sylvi. Ricus is the owner's character and the product is deliberately not soil- or garden-themed.

- No testimonials, customer counts, shop logos, usage statistics or press. None may be invented.

## Product Principles

- The counter comes first: every step should be faster than doing it in Word.
- Show the real sheet: true size, real cut marks, nothing approximate.
- Customer photos are personal data and stay on the shop's computer.
- Free and simple: no sign-up stands between a shop and its first print.
